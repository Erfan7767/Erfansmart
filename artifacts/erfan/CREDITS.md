# Erfan source credits

The reasoning constellation, golden ring composition, interaction language, and
status display are adapted from the public [APEX-UI repository](https://github.com/RubenM1990/APEX-UI)
and remain available under the MIT license. The original license is included as
`LICENSE`.

The animated WebGL plasma-wave backdrop and overview lamp panel are adaptations
of MIT-licensed community components from [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/community/components).
The original repository's `CREDITS.md` identifies them as:

- Animated shader background — `ShaderBackground.jsx`
- Overview lamp panel — `ApexOverviewPanel.tsx`

The original component authors' specific 21st.dev handles/URLs were not supplied
by the source repository, so they cannot be attributed more precisely here.

Erfan is a visual interface recreation only. It does not connect to an
autonomous-agent backend. Weather is fetched from keyless Open-Meteo after
browser location permission is granted.
